The Autonomous Expense Pipeline analysis reveals a significant underutilization of approved budgets across all business units and cost centres. This report provides a detailed breakdown of spending trends, budget variances, and cost centre observations to inform strategic decision-making.

**Key Insights**

* The highest spending business unit is Corporate Services, with actual expenses totaling $4,506,400.
* The largest expense category is Salaries, accounting for $6,612,900 of total expenses.
* No cost centres are significantly over budget (>5%).
* The largest monthly increase in expenses occurred in June 2026, with a 155.7% month-over-month (MoM) increase.

**Spending Trends**

The monthly trend analysis reveals a significant spike in expenses in June 2026, followed by a decrease in August 2026. The baseline month is January 2026, with expenses totaling $354,600. The budget variance analysis reveals a significant underutilization of approved budgets across all funding types. Temporary funding is the most underutilized, with a variance of $-7,381,300 (-57.2%). The top 10 cost centres by expense reveal a mix of business units and expense categories. Corporate Services and Financial Operations are the most significant contributors to total expenses. The largest variance is observed in CS100 (Corporate Services), with a variance of $-1,933,900 (-47.7%).
